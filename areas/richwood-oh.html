Working in Richwood

Richwood is south-west of Marion in Union County, off 37. Village and rural properties both.

Ridgeway is based in Marion, so Richwood is a normal working day rather than a special trip. That matters most on the small jobs — a leak, a length of gutter, a porch post — which are exactly the ones a contractor two counties away will not drive out for.

Roofing is the headline, but the same crew handles the siding, the windows, the porch and the deck. On an older Richwood house those things tend to arrive together.
